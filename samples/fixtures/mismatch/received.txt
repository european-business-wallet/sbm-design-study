The plaintext AS RECEIVED in the payload-hash-mismatch demonstration.

sample-NDE-mismatch.json shows a recipient that decrypted a message, recomputed
payload_hash over what it decrypted, and got a value differing from the one the
sender declared in the SE. The recipient's digest is the SHA-256 of THIS file,
so the demonstration is reproducible: a reader can hash these octets and obtain
the value the confirmation carries, and see that it is not the declared one.

In a real exchange nobody outside the two endpoints holds this text. It is
shipped here because the sample is a fixture, and because the alternative was
what stood in this field until 27 September 2026 - sixty-four f characters,
which no verifier can reproduce and which announced that the value had been
typed rather than computed.
