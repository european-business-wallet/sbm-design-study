# IPR disclosures register

This file is the public register of **patent disclosures** relevant to the Secure Business Messaging Profile. It complements the Royalty-Free (RAND-Z) patent commitment in [`IPR.md`](IPR.md): the commitment governs Contributors' Essential Patent Claims; this register records *known* patent claims (a Contributor's own, or a third party's) that a good-faith participant believes may read on the Specification, so implementers can assess them.

**Anyone** — contributor or reader — who becomes aware of a patent or published application they believe may be **essential** to implementing a mandatory (MUST/SHALL/REQUIRED) part of the Specification is asked to disclose it: open an *IPR disclosure* issue (the [`IPR disclosure`](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/ipr-disclosure.md) template) or a pull request adding a row below. Disclosing a third party's patent is **not** an admission that it is valid or infringed.

**Late disclosures.** A disclosure made after a Contribution is incorporated does not retroactively remove that Contributor's RAND-Z obligation for their own Essential Patent Claims (see [`IPR.md`](IPR.md) §1, §5); it does, however, help implementers and is always welcome.
