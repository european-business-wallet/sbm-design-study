# Patent Commitment and IPR (Royalty-Free / RAND-Z)

**Document:** Secure Business Messaging Profile
**Version of this document:** 1.0
**Applies to:** the Secure Business Messaging Profile specification set (the umbrella profile, the IETF Internet-Draft, the TS-shaped QERDS binding) and the companion artefacts in this repository.

This document holds the **patent commitment** and the **contribution IPR terms** for the project. It complements the [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) overview (which maps copyright: CC BY 4.0 for the specification text and documentation, MIT for reference code and machine-readable artefacts) and [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) (the contribution process). Copyright and patent rights are independent legal mechanisms: an implementer needs both the copyright license (CC BY 4.0) and the patent commitment below to implement the Specification freely from the published text.

## 1. Commitment

Each Contributor irrevocably grants, on a **royalty-free, non-discriminatory basis**, to any implementer of the Specification a non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual license under such Contributor's Essential Patent Claims to make, have made, use, sell, offer to sell, import and otherwise distribute Conformant Implementations of the Specification.

This commitment is commonly referred to as **RAND-Z** (Reasonable And Non-Discriminatory, Zero royalty) or equivalently **Royalty-Free**. It is modelled on the patent policies of the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C Royalty-Free Patent Policy, 1 August 2017) and the Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF, RFC 8179).

## 2. Definitions

- **Contribution** means material that is (a) **intentionally submitted for inclusion** in the Specification or its companion artefacts (for example, via a pull request, patch, or material explicitly offered for incorporation) **and** (b) **actually incorporated** into them. Material that is not intended for inclusion, or that is not incorporated, is **not** a Contribution for the purposes of this patent commitment: in particular, a bare issue, comment, review remark, or discussion post that is not incorporated is not a Contribution and does not trigger any patent obligation.
- **Contributor** means any individual or legal entity that makes a Contribution as defined above.
- **Essential Patent Claim** means any claim of a patent owned or controlled by a Contributor that would necessarily be infringed by the act of making, using, selling, offering to sell, importing or otherwise distributing a Conformant Implementation, where such infringement is unavoidable when implementing the mandatory (MUST, SHALL, REQUIRED) portions of the Specification. Claims covering optional (MAY, SHOULD, RECOMMENDED) portions are included to the extent such optional portions are actually implemented in a manner described by the Specification.
- **Conformant Implementation** means an implementation that faithfully follows the normative requirements of the Specification as they apply to the class of implementation concerned (as defined in the umbrella §9 conformance clause).

## 3. Scope and exclusions

The commitment in this document is limited to the Specification and its companion artefacts as published in this repository. It does **not** extend to:

- portions of implementations that go beyond what the Specification mandates or describes;
- external protocols and standards referenced by the Specification (including but not limited to IETF RFC 9420 on Messaging Layer Security, trust services qualified under Regulation (EU) No 910/2014, OpenID4VP, and COSE RFC 9052, and any cipher suites used therein), which are governed by their own IPR policies;
- patents owned or controlled by third parties who are not Contributors to the Specification;
- trademarks, service marks, trade names or goodwill associated with the Specification or any Contributor.

## 4. Defensive termination

If any implementer of the Specification, or any entity affiliated with such implementer, initiates patent litigation (including a cross-claim or counterclaim in a lawsuit) alleging that the Specification itself, or any Conformant Implementation thereof, directly or indirectly infringes any patent, then the licenses granted to that implementer and its affiliates under this document shall terminate automatically as of the date such litigation is filed. This is a defensive measure and does not affect the licenses held by parties not involved in the litigation.

## 5. Contribution IPR terms (inbound = outbound)

By making a **Contribution** (as defined in §2), a Contributor represents that:

1. the Contributor has the legal right to make the Contribution and to grant the licenses referenced here and in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) with respect to the Contribution;
2. the Contribution is licensed outbound on the same terms it is received — **CC BY 4.0** for specification text and documentation, and **MIT** for reference code and machine-readable artefacts, as applicable (see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE));
3. the Contributor's Essential Patent Claims are subject to the Royalty-Free (RAND-Z) commitment in §1.

This "inbound = outbound" principle ensures every Contribution carries the same rights downstream that the Contributor received from the project. The process is described in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## 6. IETF and ETSI regimes

The Internet-Draft (`ietf/draft-sbm-mls-erd-00.md`) is, in this repository, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) Part A). **Upon submission to the IETF, the submitted copy is additionally subject to the IETF Trust Legal Provisions (BCP 78 / BCP 79) per the draft's `ipr: trust200902` front-matter declaration; in case of conflict for the submitted contribution, the IETF Trust Legal Provisions govern that submitted contribution.** The TS-shaped binding is not an ETSI deliverable; upon any adoption by ETSI, the ETSI IPR Policy would govern the resulting ETSI deliverable, while the text as published here remains under CC BY 4.0.

## 7. No other rights

This document grants only the patent rights expressly described above. It does not, by implication, estoppel or otherwise, grant any other rights, licenses, or immunities.
